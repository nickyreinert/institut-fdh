Computers Are Not Punctual

2022

A comprehensive exploration from the Nerd Encyclopedia of the challenges computers face when measuring and processing time. Why Is 1 Byte 8 Bits Long?

2022

A detailed historical and technical explanation of why a byte is today standardly 8 bits in size. The article examines the origins of the term “byte”, the development of bit sizes in early computer systems, the role of ASCII and IBM in establishing the 8-bit standard, the later official standardization, and the introduction of the term “octet”.
